---
name: Cover branding
description: User direction for Rayana Bistro cover branding and copy.
---

Use a genuinely transparent Rayana Bistro logo prepared before placement, not a visible rectangular background disguised by blending.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested a transparent background before adding the logo to the cover.

**How to apply:** Preserve transparency when replacing or processing the brand emblem.

Do not reintroduce “and three-shift economics” into the cover subtitle.

**Why:** The user repeated its removal and said it adds no value.

**How to apply:** Honor this in cover copy changes and previously saved deck state.

Use the spelling “hapjes” in all logos, positioned on the upper inner ring.

**Why:** The user requested that placement and then corrected the spelling from “happjes” to “hapjes” in all logos.

**How to apply:** Use exactly HAPJES, with one P, and keep it on the inner ring in both light and dark emblems.

Remove “farm direct” from the cover logo.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested its removal.

**How to apply:** Do not restore those words when revising the emblem.

Center all cover-card text. Label the cover profit card “Year 1 Profit Estimate” and show only the conservative value.

**Why:** The user requested centered cover cards and a single conservative estimate instead of a range.

**How to apply:** Keep this presentation specific to the cover; other financial slides can retain their scenario ranges.

When masking text on the transparent logo, intersect the text mask with the original alpha channel.

**Why:** Grayscale thresholding alone treats transparent pixels as dark, which can paint opaque rectangular patches over the gaps between rings.

**How to apply:** Preserve the original transparent pixels during raster text retouching.
