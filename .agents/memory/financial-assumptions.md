Do not invent basket splits or operating costs for the business. Break-even requires a food/drink spending split and per-shift staffing plus allocated overhead; a gross-margin range alone cannot establish it.

**Why:** The deck's original guest counts and average checks do not establish those missing business assumptions. Supplying plausible-looking defaults would present unsupported numbers as facts.

**How to apply:** Leave unknown assumptions unset until entered, distinguish unset values from explicit zero, and identify the food/drink margins used. Staffing and overhead inputs must exclude ingredient costs already covered by gross margins.

## User-supplied planning baseline

Assume rent plus utilities of €3,000/month and 50 guests for each shift.

**Why:** The user supplied these figures for the planning scenario, not as verified lease or sales data.

**How to apply:** Keep rent/utilities separate from insurance, administration and other costs. Any proposed staffing allowances or food/drink splits remain estimates unless the user confirms them.
